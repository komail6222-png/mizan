RASID: decision quality, not only forecast error

Share of the achievable battery value each policy captured

0% is running without a battery, 100% is the hindsight reference that plans each day on the realized load.

Expected cost against tail cost

Tail cost is the mean of the worst 10% of days (empirical CVaR90). Down is safer, left is cheaper. The no-battery policy is off this scale.

Paired differences in mean daily cost

95% moving-block bootstrap interval, 7-day blocks. An interval that crosses zero is not resolved by these 60 days.
